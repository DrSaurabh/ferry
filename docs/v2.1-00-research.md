# Ferry 2.1 — A0 research: what users of similar apps want (2026-11-23, night run 1)

Sources skimmed (store reviews, forums, articles): LocalSend (issues, reviews), Amazon Fire TV remote app reviews and AFTVnews coverage, third-party Fire TV remote apps, TV Cast / Web Video Caster reviews, photo-transfer apps (Photo Transfer App, PhotoSync, appuals guide on timestamps), Fire TV / Apple TV slideshow apps, Emby/Plex/Kodi slideshow threads.

## What users praise or complain about → what it means for Ferry

| # | Finding (paraphrased) | Source type | Ferry decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Amazon's phone remote **disconnects whenever the phone screen turns off** and is sometimes hard to reconnect; "keeps disconnecting, has to be restarted" | Fire TV app store reviews | **Adopt**: controller reconnects silently and instantly when the phone wakes or the app returns; controls also in the notification / lock screen while something plays (F35, F24-friendly: only while playing) |
| R2 | Users repeatedly ask for **volume** in phone remote apps; switching between phone and physical remote for volume is annoying | Fire TV app reviews | **Adopt** (already wanted, F35): volume −/+ and mute on the controller (stick media volume) |
| R3 | The old **swipe/gesture area** was easier than buttons because you can use it **without looking at the phone** | AFTVnews on the Fire TV app redesign | **Adopt**: controller has a large touch pad (swipe = arrows, tap = OK, two-finger/pinch = zoom when zoomable) plus buttons |
| R4 | Typing with the **phone keyboard** is the most-loved remote feature | many remote apps | Already planned (F35) |
| R5 | Users resent **scrubbing/seek** being limited; want to drag to any point | TV Cast reviews | Already planned: seek bar with times (F35) |
| R6 | A synced playbar so **several phones in the room control the same TV** | TV Cast feature | **Defer to Stage B** (F34 multi-device) |
| R7 | Casting apps "don't connect 50% of the time" — connection reliability is the #1 complaint | TV Cast / remote reviews | **Adopt** as logging focus: every connect attempt, result and reconnect is logged (F30 distress detector "controller command sent, TV didn't react") |
| R8 | "Your device must be able to decode the video; no transcoding" — users don't understand why a video fails | Web Video Caster FAQ | Matches F16: phone checks the TV's decoder limits first and says why in plain words |
| R9 | Slideshow wishes: **date/time overlay** on photos, **shuffle**, smooth **fade** transitions, captions; Ken Burns effect | Amazon Photos, Plex/Emby/Kodi threads, slideshow apps | **Adopt** shuffle (off by default), optional date-taken line (off by default), simple cross-fade. **Reject** Ken Burns for now (heavy on a 1st-gen stick; rule 12) |
| R10 | Annoyance: **zoom resets** unexpectedly in a slideshow app | Shuffle Photos review | **Adopt**: zooming pauses the slideshow; zoom stays until the user leaves the photo or presses Back; next photo starts unzoomed |
| R11 | Photo transfer users care most that **date taken and location survive** and dislike **duplicates** | PhotoSync/Photo Transfer reviews, appuals, MS Q&A | Already core (P2, P5); F7 logging makes it visible |
| R12 | Imported files organised into **year/month folders** | photo-transfer article | **Defer to Stage B** (F26 destination folders: option "sort into year/month") |
| R13 | LocalSend: rename or drop files **before** sending; **favourite devices**; send a **text/link** | LocalSend reviews/changelog | Drop-before-send: covered by the picker/selection review. Favourites: Stage B choosers. Text/link: **backlog** (not in 2.1) |
| R14 | Remote-app users want the remote to **find the TV automatically**, with a manual address fallback | remote app descriptions | Already in 2.0 (discovery + Enter TV address) |

## Net additions to Stage A scope
R1 (silent reconnect + playback notification), R3 (touch pad), R9 (shuffle, date line, cross-fade), R10 (zoom pauses slideshow), R7 (connection logging). R2/R4/R5/R8 confirm planned items. R6, R12, R13 → Stage B / backlog.
