## 3. Phone: sending and viewing
| ID | Requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| P11 | **Thumbnails** on every row of the transfer screen (photos and videos). | F8 |
| P12 | **Preview before sending / casting**: in the picker and on Play on TV, a ▶ on a video (or long-press on any item) opens a full-screen preview (video plays with sound; photo zoomable); Back returns with picks intact. The preview shows resolution and length and the U7 warning when a TV is known. | F22 |
| P13 | **Sends wait for the PC**: a send whose PC is not reachable (Ferry closed, PC off, Wi-Fi away) is kept: "Waiting for <PC> — will send when Ferry is running there". It resumes by itself when the PC is found, also after the app was closed or the phone restarted. Items shared from other apps are copied into Ferry's private storage while waiting (deleted after sending); picker items are kept by reference. The user can cancel the waiting send. Files still restart from their beginning; no partial files on the PC. | F20, V1, V4 |
| P14 | **Battery rule — nothing of Ferry runs when not in use** [H thresholds]: the transfer service stops the moment a send ends; while waiting for the PC (P13) the foreground service runs at most **10 min**, then a background check (WorkManager: on network change and every ~15 min, battery-not-low) takes over; discovery retries run only while a Ferry screen is visible and back off after 1 min; a Play-on-TV/controller session ends after **10 min** with nothing playing; wake/Wi-Fi locks only during actual transfers or while serving a playing video. | F24, V8 |

## 6. Logging and the "normal use" testing approach (F7, F10, F12, F30; V11)
| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| L1 | **Usage log** on phone and TV: one structured line per event (time, session id, device, event, key=value details): app start/stop/background, screen shown, button/key pressed (by id), Back, picks, permission asked/result, send start/each file result (size, time, speed)/finish, waits/retries/resumes, service start/stop, locks held, discovery attempts/results, connection to TV/PC, controller commands sent/received/acknowledged, player events (play, pause, seek, error with decoder/format details, first frame time), zoom, slideshow steps, errors shown to the user with their exact text. No photo content; file names allowed. |
| L2 | **Detail level** setting (phone Settings and PC): Normal (default) and **Detailed** (everything in L1 plus per-request detail). Detailed is the default **during the 2.1 trial** [H]: a "Trial logging" switch on, which the user can turn off. |
| L3 | **Automatic log collection**: phone and TV upload their new log lines to the PC whenever connected (at most every 10 min and at app background), into `<Ferry folder>\logs\<device name>\`. PC keeps `ferry-log.txt` there too (moved into `logs\`). Nothing leaves the home network. Rotation/size cap: phone/TV ≤ 5 MB on device; PC folder ≤ 200 MB, oldest removed. |
| L4 | **"Something's wrong" button**: phone — in the top bar overflow and in the notification while sending/playing; one tap logs a marker with the current screen and optional short note (dialog with a text field, "Skip" allowed). TV — long-press Back... [H: final key in design] logs a marker and shows "Noted". |
| L5 | **PC log content** (F7/F10): per received file one readable line ("saved IMG_1.jpg · 4.2 MB in 0.2 s · 21 MB/s · date taken 2025-07-03 17:28 · GPS yes · from vivo X200 Pro"); per batch a summary line; JPEG EXIF date/GPS read by the PC (stdlib); for HEIC/videos the phone sends what it read (date taken, GPS present, original vs location-stripped) as upload headers and the PC logs them. |
| L6 | **Log review script** (`tools/review_logs.py`, run by Claude on an uploaded logs zip): merges device logs by time, and lists **distress signals**: action started with no result; same button ≥3× within 5 s; screen A→B→A within 10 s; same dialog/error twice in a row; Back that discarded picks; app backgrounded or killed mid-task; video stopped < 10 s after start; waits > 30 s; controller command without TV acknowledgement; permission refusals; every "Something's wrong" marker with 30 s of context. Output: a readable report. |
| L7 | One PC action "Zip logs for Claude" (key in the PC window) creates `ferry-logs-<date>.zip` in the logs folder. |
