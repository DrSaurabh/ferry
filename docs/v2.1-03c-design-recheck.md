# Ferry 2.1 — A3 independent re-check of the design fixes (checker: Sonnet, 2026-11-24)

Scope: `git diff a0c92b4 HEAD -- docs/` (design §13 M1–M24, requirement P14, decisions H77–H90) against the audit findings S1–S16, O1–O17, the requirements (rev 1 + P14 change), VISION and the existing code. Verified cheaply: grep of `pc/ferry.py` and `android/` (routes, notification actions, tests that mention Play on TV, TV update banner), a Python run of the Fire TV UA regex. Android platform points not run on a device are marked **uncertain**.

Overall: the amendments resolve nearly everything. 28 of 33 are fully resolved and 5 are resolved except for a small residual (listed), none unresolved. No High findings. The main risks introduced are: a notification with more actions than Android shows (C1), requirements left stale (C2), a cast session that can end while the TV is still using it (C4).

## C-findings

### C1 — Medium — M17 + M22 vs R9: plain notification with 5–6 actions (technical)
M17 says lock-screen/notification controls are "plain notification actions, not a MediaSession". R9 needs play/pause, −10, +10, next, stop (5) and M22 adds "Something's wrong" (6). Android's standard notification layout shows at most **3 actions**; extra ones are dropped (framework `MAX_ACTION_BUTTONS = 3`; high confidence, not run). Today's cast notification (`Notifications.kt` l.156–159) has only 3 (toggle, next, stop), so this would be new breakage. `NotificationCompat.MediaStyle` (androidx.media) shows 5 in the expanded view and works without a session token, but 6 still doesn't fit.
Fix (smallest): use `MediaStyle` with 5 transport actions and put "Something's wrong" in the app's overflow only (or drop it from cast notifications; L4 asks for it "while sending/playing", so keep it on the send notification, which has room: Cancel + Wrong). Change M17's "not a MediaSession" wording to "MediaStyle without session".

### C2 — Medium — Requirements not updated to match the amendments (only P14 was)
Builders and CI tests derive from the requirements; these now disagree with the design:
- **B2** still prescribes the row text "Sent without location — the sharing app removed it" for every unresolved original. M4/H86 replaced it with evidence-gated wording and added "Original sent" / "Sent as the sharing app gave it" rows. H70 text in design §5.2 even says "B2 wording updated" but it was not.
- **B4** says "`/app` redirects TV user agents" and "`/app` page"; M11 says `/` is the page, `/app` is the APK, `/tv` and `/` redirect.
- **L2** asks for the Detailed/Normal setting "on phone Settings and PC"; M18/H89: PC has none.
- **L3** folder `logs\<device name>\` vs M12 `logs\<name> (id)\`; **L6** rules unnamed (fine) but **P13** quotes the notification text "will send when Ferry is running there" vs M5 "checks about every 15 minutes".
- **R1/R7/R11** say "TV entry"/"Play on TV" (consistent with M21), no issue.
- **A2/§0 header** "Ferry 2.1.0" vs M16 versionName `2.1.$run` (see C8).
Fix: one requirements rev 3 pass changing those lines (a few minutes) and log it. Not doing it risks a builder implementing the old B2 text or a test asserting `/app` page.

### C3 — Low — M21 removes the Play on TV button but names no consequences
Existing tests drive that button: `CastLoopbackTest.kt` (l.77, 103) and `PhoneScreenshotTour.kt` (l.54, 86); requirements §9 says existing 2.0 tests stay green, and the guide screenshots/`A3` text use it. M21 also doesn't say whether the single **TV** button is always visible (the old §2 said the remote button is shown only "when a TV was ever found or one is found now"; Play on TV was always shown). With no TV ever on the network an always-visible TV button leads to "TV not found", which is fine, but a hidden one would break first-time discovery of the feature (V3). Fix: one sentence: TV is always visible; update those tests/tour; the old tests are adapted rather than "unchanged".

### C4 — Medium — M17: a cast session can end while the TV is still showing phone-served content
M17: "every phone cast session ends after 10 min with nothing playing"; locks only while a video plays. A **photo** cast (phone's `CastFileServer` serves each photo on demand) has no "playing" state when the slideshow is off or paused; the user may sit on one photo, or a paused video, for 10 min, then the phone service stops and the TV can no longer fetch the next photo/seek (it shows an error), contradicting V5/V6. Unspecified: what the TV does when the phone service ends (go to its home? keep showing the already-loaded item?), and whether "playing" for photos means slideshow-running or any interaction in the last 10 min.
Fix: define idle as "no play/pause/next/seek/key activity for 10 min and not playing", on stop the phone sends `stop` (TV returns to its home) and logs `svc.stop reason=idle`; add a unit test (O1 asked for one; M17 doesn't mention it).

### C5 — Low — M20: "tap = OK" in R2 is now "tap the centre"
R2: "tap = OK; usable without looking". M20: edge arrows are tappable and OK is the centre. A blind tap near an edge now sends an arrow (in a menu, moves focus instead of selecting). Fix: say how big the centre OK zone is (e.g. central 60% of both axes) and keep the edge arrows visibly drawn; or keep tap-anywhere = OK and make the edge arrows small buttons outside the swipe area. Also add a gesture-classifier unit test for the zones.

### C6 — Low — M9: the TV counts as "the first phone"
`X-Ferry-Device` is also sent by the TV app. If the TV is installed first (a friend following the guide TV-first), the QR disappears before any phone connected; U6 says "first phone". The PC already tells TV from phone (`AFT` in UA / `device_type`). Fix: count only non-TV Ferry requests.

### C7 — Low (uncertain) — M5/M14: restarting the foreground service after an Android 15 `dataSync` timeout
On Android 15+ a `dataSync` FGS that hit its ~6 h/24 h budget cannot be started again from the background until the user opens the app (`ForegroundServiceStartNotAllowedException`; from memory, not verified). M14 says the worker resumes the send; that start would fail. The existing fallback ("PC is back — tap to send" notification, H71) should explicitly cover a refused start. Also note `Service.onTimeout` has the signature `onTimeout(int startId, int fgsType)` on API 35 (the one-arg form is 34-style); builder should use the one that compiles.

### C8 — Low — M16 vs A2/requirements: versionName `2.1.$run` but About/state say "2.1.0"
A2 prints "Ferry 2.1.0", TV state example `"app":"2.1.0"`, PC 2.1.0. The About text must come from `BuildConfig.VERSION_NAME` (not a literal) or the About string and the installed version will disagree and the update comparison may confuse. One line in M16.

### C9 — Low — M17: who is the single poller when there is no CastService?
With follow-only dropped, a PC-outbox `open` has no phone service. M17 says the controller "reads the same CastService/one CastClient poller at 1 s". For PC-sourced sessions the poller is `ControllerViewModel`'s `CastClient` (§2). State this (owner = service while a cast runs, else the ViewModel; hidden + no cast = no polling), otherwise two pollers can come back.

### C10 — Low — M24: the "old TV" message points at a button that isn't named that
M24: "On the TV, open Ferry and choose **Install update**." The 2.0 TV home shows `UpdateBanner` with "New Ferry X is ready → Update" (`strings.xml` l.98, `TvHomeScreen.kt` l.158), and only when the PC has a newer APK. Reword to "…choose **Update**" or "see the guide: updating the TV".

### C11 — Low — M4: residual — B2 may still be unfixed for re-encoded copies
Step 2 now needs name **and size** equal **and** date taken equal and one candidate. If the vivo gallery hands over a re-encoded copy, size differs and the share is sent as shared (with the evidence-gated note), i.e. F11's original-with-location goal isn't met in exactly that case; the design relies on the `share.resolve` log to learn which form vivo uses. That is a reasonable stance, but state it in H70/M4 as "B2 is judged from the first real logs" (as M2 does for B3) so it isn't read as proven. Also **uncertain**: Android 13+ photo-picker URIs (`content://media/picker/...`) — whether their trailing id equals the MediaStore id for step 1.

### C12 — Low — Mild over-engineering (rule 12), none blocking
Each item below has a requirement behind it, so I only flag, not reject: M2 ships a built, unit-tested but disabled `RotationTransform` behind `MANUAL_ROTATION=false` (dead code until a log says otherwise; acceptable because the 1st-gen stick can't be tested in CI); M5's 3-day / 24 h "Keep waiting / Cancel" notification state machine (needs its own receiver and timers; O15 asked for it, but "queue kept, periodic stops after 3 days, re-armed at next app open" alone would be simpler and the notification could be dropped); M12 per-install id in folder names; M15's staged CI job (needed for the kill test). Suggest deferring the 3-day notification if the builder finds it costly.

### C13 — Low — M17/M18: tests and the catalogue file are promised but not specified
`docs/v2.1-log-events.md` does not exist yet and M18 gives about seven example rows ("e.g."); the script's rule quality depends on the full catalogue, so make writing it an explicit first build step with the review script's tests reading it. M17 omits the unit test that O1 asked for on the idle rule and lock release.

### C14 — Low — M1: free-space check is per item
"free space > size + 500 MB" per item: sharing 30 videos of 2 GB each passes item by item while the total doesn't fit, and a copy then fails mid-way. Check against the remaining total (or stop copying and mark the remaining rows "Share again when the PC is on"), and delete a half-written copy.

### C15 — Low (uncertain) — M23/H85: TV device name claim not verified
"Fire OS sets a real name, e.g. Saurabh's FireTVStick" (also in the §1.3 example and H85) is asserted, not checked. If `Settings.Global.DEVICE_NAME` is empty on the 1st-gen stick, the PC shows "Amazon AFT…" (the F6 complaint again, on the TV). The `device_name src=` log (M18) will show this; state the TV fallback (e.g. "Fire TV") so the name is never a model code.

### New issues
- **D1 — Low/Medium — requirement L4 not updated.** L4 still says the phone "Something's wrong" is in the top-bar overflow **and in the notification while sending/playing". After M25 the cast notification has none (send notification and controller overflow only). Reword L4 (and H84's "in phone notifications"). Otherwise a test or the review of L4 expects a button that no longer exists.
- **D2 — Low — waiting notification becomes untrue after day 3.** With the 3-day notification dropped, the periodic checks stop but the quiet notification still says "checks about every 15 minutes". Fix: at day 3 the worker's last run updates it to "Paused — open Ferry to keep waiting for <PC> · Cancel" (V5). The Home banner alone is not enough because the user may never open Ferry.
- **D3 — Low — guide section missing.** C10's message points to the guide's "Updating the TV" steps, but the A3 guide list has no such section. Add it to A3 (or reword to the PC window's TV address).
- **D4 — Low (trivial) — C5 wording.** "Tap anywhere on the pad = OK" and "arrows act on a tap inside the edge band" conflict as written; say "tap in the middle = OK, tap on a drawn edge arrow = that arrow" in M25/H93.
