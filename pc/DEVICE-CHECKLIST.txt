FERRY 2 - DEVICE CHECKLIST (about 45 minutes)
Things the automatic tests can't check, because they need your real phone, Fire TV,
PC and Wi-Fi. Do them in order. Write the result next to each item ("OK", or what
happened instead) and send this file back. "Expected" is what should happen.
If something goes wrong, also send ferry-log.txt (Ferry folder on the PC) and the
phone's log (Ferry > Settings > Share diagnostics log).

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. PC (Windows)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1. Unzip Ferry (README section 1) and double-click Ferry.bat.
    Expected: Windows Firewall asks about "Python"; after "Allow access" the window
    shows a QR code and "App: Ferry 2.0.x is ready for your phone and TV".
    Result: ______
A2. Double-click "Ferry self-test.bat".
    Expected: "ALL PASSED". It saves ferry-selftest.txt - send it.   Result: ______

---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B. PHONE (vivo): install and first use
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
B1. Install the app from the QR code (README section 2). Note every warning or question
    the phone shows (Chrome, Play Protect, vivo), in order.
    Result: ______________________________________________
B2. Open Ferry. Welcome screen > battery card > Allow > Continue.
    Expected: Home says "Connected to <your PC>" within a few seconds, without typing
    anything (Ferry finds the PC by itself).                         Result: ______
B3. Do the vivo battery steps (README section 4). Do the menu names match your phone?
    If not, write what they are called:  ______________________________________

C. PHONE -> PC  (acceptance 1, 2, 3)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C1. Send photos & videos: pick 10 photos in one album and 10 in another (Albums tab),
    some taken with location on. Send.
    Expected: "20 saved". On the PC, in Downloads\Phone: same names; Windows shows the
    date taken; right-click a photo > Properties > Details shows GPS.
    Result: ______
C2. Send the same 20 photos again.
    Expected: "20 already on PC", nothing is copied again.           Result: ______
C3. Send one video of 1 GB or more. After it starts, turn the screen off and leave the
    phone for 5 minutes. Then turn it on.
    Expected: the video is saved on the PC (same size).               Result: ______
    Speed shown with the screen on: ______ MB/s   with the screen off: ______ MB/s
C4. Send about 100 photos at once. Note the speed shown.
    Expected (hope): 10 MB/s or more.                     Speed: ______ MB/s
C5. In the vivo Gallery: select 3 photos > Share > Ferry.
    Expected: a short message "Sending 3 files to <your PC>", you stay in the Gallery,
    the 3 photos arrive on the PC with their location.               Result: ______
C6. While a big video is sending, switch the PC's Wi-Fi off (or unplug its network)
    for 1 minute, then on again.
    Expected: the phone shows "Waiting for <your PC>..." and carries on by itself; no
    half file on the PC.                                             Result: ______
